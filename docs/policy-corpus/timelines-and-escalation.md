# Timelines and escalation (sample)

Sample corpus for demo — replace with official documents.

## Disposal timeline

Each case carries an expected disposal date set at intake. The deadline tracker runs hourly and moves the case from
on track, to nearing, to overdue as that date approaches and passes.

## Reminders

Officers and college management receive a reminder when a case is nearing its deadline and again when it is overdue.
Reminders are sent once per day per case.

## Escalation

An officer may escalate a case that is stuck or overdue. Escalation keeps the current status and records an event
with the reason. It does not transfer the case. A bureau head decides on any transfer.

## Transfer between bureaus

A case can be transferred to another bureau only by a bureau head. The prior bureau is recorded so that the history
stays complete.
