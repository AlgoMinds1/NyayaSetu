# Appeals (sample)

Sample corpus for demo — replace with official documents.

## Eligibility

When a case is closed, the complainant becomes eligible to appeal. The appeal window is recorded as eligible at the
moment of closure.

## Filing an appeal

The complainant submits the appeal through the portal with the reason for it. The case then moves to appeal
submitted and goes to the ombudsperson.

## Decision

The ombudsperson reviews the appeal and records a decision. The decision can confirm the earlier closure or send the
case back for examination.
