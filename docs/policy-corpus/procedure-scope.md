# Scope of this assistant (sample)

Sample corpus for demo — replace with official documents.

## What the assistant answers

The assistant explains procedure: how a complaint is filed, how it moves through triage, what the timelines are,
how escalation and appeals work, and who handles each step.

## What the assistant does not answer

The assistant does not decide whether a specific complaint is valid, well-founded or likely to succeed. That is a
decision for the case officer, based on the evidence on file.
